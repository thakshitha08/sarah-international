Excavator Attachments

Engineered for strength. Built for performance. Trusted by professionals across construction, mining, demolition, and infrastructure projects. Sarah International manufactures high-quality excavator attachments designed to deliver maximum durability, efficiency, and reliability in the toughest working environments.

Fast · Safe · Reliable

Quick Couplers

Our quick couplers are engineered to help operators switch attachments quickly and safely, reducing downtime and improving jobsite productivity. Built with high-strength steel and precision-machined locking systems, they ensure a secure fit and long service life.

  • Hydraulic and mechanical options
  • Fail-safe locking mechanisms
  • High compatibility with major excavator brands
  • Reinforced structure for heavy-duty applications
  • Designed for fast, safe attachment changes

Best for: contractors who need rapid transitions between buckets, breakers, rippers, and other attachments.

Precision Handling for Tough Materials

Grabs & Grapples

Our grabs and grapples are designed for maximum grip strength, stability, and control. Whether handling scrap, demolition debris, timber, or bulk materials, these attachments deliver consistent performance in demanding environments.

  • Demolition & sorting grabs
  • Orange peel grapples
  • Timber grabs
  • Multi-purpose hydraulic grabs

Advantages: high-strength tines and wear-resistant edges, smooth hydraulic operation, excellent rotation and control, built for continuous heavy-duty use.

Built to Dig Deeper and Last Longer

Buckets

Our buckets are fabricated using abrasion-resistant steel, reinforced side plates, and precision welding to withstand extreme conditions. Each bucket is engineered for optimal digging force, breakout power, and material flow.

  • General purpose buckets — for everyday digging and loading
  • Heavy-duty rock buckets — reinforced for quarrying and demolition
  • Trenching buckets — narrow, efficient, and precise
  • Tilt buckets — ideal for grading and shaping

Performance benefits: optimized digging geometry, high breakout force, extended wear life, smooth material flow.
