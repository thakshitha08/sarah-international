About Sarah International

Precision. Innovation. Reliability.

A single manufacturing standard across hydraulics, attachments, drilling equipment, and contract fabrication — built to keep heavy machinery running.

Who We Are

Engineering expertise, manufacturing scale

Sarah International combines deep industry experience, robust production capacity, and strict safety and quality controls to deliver structural, industrial, and precision metal components that meet your specifications and schedule. We serve customers across construction, mining, quarrying, agriculture, manufacturing, and material handling with four integrated divisions: hydraulic cylinders, excavator attachments, drilling solutions, and contract heavy-metal fabrication.

Our team of skilled engineers and certified welders ensures every product is crafted with attention to detail and in compliance with international standards, from a single prototype run to high-volume, repeatable production.
